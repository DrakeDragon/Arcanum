PUT YOUR OWN ARTWORK IN THIS FOLDER

Names (extension can be .jpg, .png, .webp or .jpeg):

  art/back.jpg                 the card back (one image for every face-down card)
  art/hero.jpg                 optional wide banner at the top of Home (16:9)
  art/cards/00.jpg ... 77.jpg  the 78 cards. 00-21 Major Arcana (The Fool = 00, The World = 21),
                               22-35 Wands (Ace to King), 36-49 Cups, 50-63 Swords, 64-77 Pentacles
  art/signs/aries.jpg ...      the 12 zodiac signs, lowercase (aries, taurus, gemini, cancer, leo, virgo,
                               libra, scorpio, sagittarius, capricorn, aquarius, pisces)
  art/animals/rat.jpg ...      the 12 Chinese animals, lowercase (rat, ox, tiger, rabbit, dragon, snake,
                               horse, goat, monkey, rooster, dog, pig)

Then open the app: Settings > Art Studio > turn on "Use my art".
Images that are missing keep the built-in drawings, so you can add them a few at a time.
Card images: portrait 2:3 (for example 1024 x 1536), under about 500 KB each.
ART_GUIDE.txt (one folder up) has a ready-made ChatGPT prompt for every image.
